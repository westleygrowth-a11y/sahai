New organization? Start here.

The CEO completes Initial CEO setup once, using the private setup token configured in Railway. Do not share that token with employees. Other users sign in with accounts created by the CEO; they do not repeat CEO setup.

Then follow the CEO setup steps below before inviting the team. If setup already says configured, use Sign in instead.

YOUR ROLE

CEO

Set direction. Keep ownership clear.

Step 1 · Set up your team once
  1. After initial CEO setup, sign in with your CEO email and password. Setup closes after that first account is created.
  2. Open Organization. Use Add location for institutions or campuses and Add department for the department structure.
  3. Use Add employee to create directors, department heads, supervisors, and employees. Set the job title, authorization role, institution, department, campus, and Reports to.
  4. Create managers before their reports. Both directors report to the CEO. Give delegation and restricted HR or finance access only where needed. Share initial passwords privately.
Step 2 · Assign an outcome
  1. Click New task. Write a clear Task title and Detailed instructions explaining the result you expect.
  2. Choose the Assigned executor, Due date & time, Priority, Record classification, and Required evidence, if needed. All deadlines use Asia/Karachi (PKT).
  3. Click Create task. If you select Save as draft without notifying the executor, open it later and use Publish assignment when ready.
  4. Example: Assign “Prepare campus readiness report” to the Operations Director. Request a checklist and photos, with a clear deadline.
Step 3 · Check progress without taking over
  1. Open Overview. Check Overdue, Blocked, Awaiting review, and Priority attention.
  2. Open Tasks → Tree to see how an assignment has been delegated. The original accountable owner stays responsible.
  3. Open an assignment and use Request an update when an authorized action is available. A direct CEO assignment does not change the employee’s formal manager.
  4. Review the task history and evidence. For submitted work you are authorized to review, use Approve completion or Request corrections with a clear note. You cannot approve your own execution.
Step 4 · Confirm the working calendar
  1. Open Settings and confirm working days, hours, holidays, reminders, and escalation intervals.
  2. Automation is a rules-based simulation. Run follow-up simulation is available to the CEO; scheduled checks also require a running worker service.
  3. Notifications stay inside SAH AI. No live AI, WhatsApp, email, or attendance-device integration is active.

Your daily checklist

Quick practice · If a delegated child finishes, is the director’s parent assignment automatically complete?

No. Each parent must be submitted and separately approved after its mandatory children are completed.

YOUR ROLE

Director

Turn an outcome into coordinated work.

Step 1 · Know your scope
  1. Finance Director oversees Accounts. Operations Director oversees Academic, HR & Administration, Admissions, and IT. Both report to the CEO.
  2. Overview shows work within your authorized scope. Restricted HR and finance records also need explicit access.
  3. If a person or action is missing, ask the CEO to check the reporting line, active account, and permission grants.
Step 2 · Receive and start an assignment
  1. Open Tasks and click the task title. Read Detailed instructions, Required evidence, the deadline, and the accountable owner.
  2. Under Update assignment, select Acknowledged, add a note, and click Save update.
  3. When work starts, select In Progress, add a note, and click Save update.
Step 3 · Delegate clear pieces of work
  1. When you have delegation permission, open the task and click Delegate.
  2. Choose an authorized department head, explain the deliverable, and set a child deadline no later than the parent deadline.
  3. Keep Mandatory subtask before parent completion checked when that result is required. Click Delegate subtask.
  4. Example: For campus readiness, delegate academic checks to the Principal and facilities checks to the HR & Administration Head. Use Tasks → Tree to track the pieces.
Step 4 · Review, then report upward
  1. Review submitted child tasks you are authorized to manage. Check evidence, then Approve completion or Request corrections with a reason.
  2. Ensure all mandatory child tasks are Completed. Child completion does not automatically close your assignment.
  3. Upload your required evidence, then choose Submitted for Review, explain the result, and Save update. A separate authorized reviewer approves the parent.

Your daily checklist

Quick practice · Does a director automatically have access to every restricted finance or HR task?

No. Visibility follows reporting scope and the required explicit sensitive-record access grants.

YOUR ROLE

Department manager

Organize the work. Help your team finish.

Step 1 · Find your team’s work
  1. Sign in and open Overview or Tasks. Search by task title or owner; filter by department or status.
  2. Use List for deadlines, Tree for parent and child relationships, and Kanban for status columns.
  3. Principals use the Academic Department Head role. Maintenance reports through HR & Administration → Maintenance Supervisor → Technicians.
Step 2 · Accept and plan
  1. Open your assigned task, read the instructions, then save Acknowledged with a note.
  2. Save In Progress when you start. Confirm the expected evidence and the parent deadline.
  3. With an explicit delegation grant, click Delegate to create subtasks for authorized reports. Set clear instructions and deadlines within the parent deadline.
Step 3 · Support and review your team
  1. Use Request an update when available. Read the employee’s history and specific blockers.
  2. When work is submitted, check the evidence and completion requirements. Choose Approve completion or Request corrections if you are an authorized separate reviewer.
  3. A job title alone does not grant delegation or review authority. If a control is absent, ask the CEO to check permissions.
Step 4 · Complete your own assignment
  1. Wait for all mandatory child tasks to be Completed.
  2. Upload any evidence required on your own parent assignment. Add a brief summary of results.
  3. Choose Submitted for Review and Save update. Your own assignment still needs approval from a separate authorized reviewer.

Your daily checklist

Quick practice · Your team has finished its mandatory child tasks. What do you do next?

Check the result, upload evidence required on your parent task, and submit that parent for review. It does not finish automatically.

YOUR ROLE

Employee

Know what to do. Show what you completed.

Step 1 · Open your assignment
  1. Sign in with the email and initial password provided privately by your organization.
  2. Open Tasks and click the task title, or use Notifications → Open assignment.
  3. Read the instructions, deadline in PKT, priority, and required evidence. Ask a question using Post an update / comment if something is unclear.
Step 2 · Acknowledge and start
  1. In Update assignment → Action, choose Acknowledged. Add a short note such as “I understand the request.” Click Save update.
  2. When you begin, choose In Progress, add a short progress note, and Save update.
  3. Use Post an update / comment for factual progress. A comment by itself does not change the task status.
Step 3 · Ask for help early
  1. If you cannot continue, choose Blocked and explain the obstacle, the help needed, and the next step.
  2. After the obstacle is resolved, choose In Progress and save a note.
  3. If you need more time, choose Request deadline extension, enter the requested deadline, explain why, and save. A request does not itself change the approved deadline.
Step 4 · Show the result and submit
  1. If evidence is required, use Upload evidence before submitting. Supported files are PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, and TXT, up to 2 MB each.
  2. Choose Submitted for Review, describe what you completed, and click Save update.
  3. If the reviewer requests corrections, read the feedback, fix the work, upload updated evidence if needed, and resubmit.
  4. Completed means a separate authorized reviewer has approved the work. Employees cannot approve their own execution. Delegation is available only with an explicit grant.

Your daily checklist

Quick practice · You posted “Done” as a comment. Is the task completed?

No. Upload required evidence, use Submitted for Review, and wait for a separate authorized reviewer to approve it.

The normal task journey

Assigned → Acknowledged → In Progress → Submitted for Review → Completed

Draft: not yet published; private to its creator and the CEO. Blocked: work cannot continue; explain why. Changes Requested: the reviewer needs corrections. Cancelled: the task has been stopped by an authorized manager.

Completed and Cancelled tasks cannot be reopened through the current workflow. History is kept; comments do not erase earlier updates.

Who stays responsible?

The originating requester asked for the result. The accountable owner retains original responsibility. The executor performs the current assignment. Delegating creates a linked child task; it does not rewrite formal reporting lines or transfer the original accountability.

Use the right screen

  • Overview: active, overdue, blocked, and awaiting-review work in your scope.
  • Tasks: search and filter; switch between List, Tree, and Kanban.
  • Organization: see reporting lines. The CEO manages employee accounts and grants.
  • Notifications: read internal messages and open linked assignments.
  • Automation: rules-based simulation and follow-up information.
  • Settings: account information and sign-out; CEO follow-up policy controls.
A button, person, or task is missing

Clear task filters first. Actions depend on your role, explicit grants, reporting scope, task status, and restricted-record access. Ask the CEO to review the account settings. A technical administrator does not automatically have access to operational tasks.

An update could not be saved

Read the error, reopen the task to load its latest state, and check whether someone else changed it. Check required notes, evidence, mandatory children, permissions, and deadline limits before retrying. If the error persists, contact your administrator with the task title and error text; do not send passwords.

What is available now?

Available: secure sign-in, organization setup, scoped task assignment and delegation, comments and history, evidence, review, deadline requests, in-app notifications, and simulated briefings/follow-ups.

Scheduled reminders require a configured running worker. Live AI agents, employee email/WhatsApp messaging, attendance devices, and dedicated maintenance modules are not active in this release. Maintenance staff can use the existing authorized task workflow.
